What is molecular hydrogen?

Molecular hydrogen (H₂) is two hydrogen atoms bonded together — the smallest molecule there is. It acts as a selective antioxidant, and its tiny size lets it move easily into cells.*

How do I take the tablets?

Drop 1 tablet into a glass of cold, still water once a day. Wait until it has fully dissolved, then drink right away. Hydrogen gradually escapes from open water, so fresh is best.

When is the best time to drink it?

Whenever it fits your routine. Many people make it their first glass of the morning; others use it as an afternoon reset.

What does it taste like?

Unflavored tastes close to plain water. Raspberry adds a light fruit flavor. Both are sugar-free and calorie-free.
